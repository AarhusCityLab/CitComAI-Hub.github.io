## Overview

​GovTech Midtjylland (GTM) and the City of Aarhus provides an opportunity for AI innovators to test and experiment in a real life setting in a modern city.​
Aarhus City Lab is a one stop shop for real-life testing of new technology within the domain of Smart City and is also a part of the local EDIH.​

Core CitCom services:​

Robot test in public environment – both early and late stage robots​
Access to existing SmartCity data for AI innovation​
Create a custom IoT SmartCity dataset for AI innovation based on new sensor data​
Initiating public sector collaboration in order to strengthen use cases and business cases for AI and robot solutions​

Valuable capabilities in Aarhus includes a high precision GNSS-system and LoRaWan network and IoT-platform that can be made available for CitCom customers.

- **Service 1**: SERVICE #1: Robot test in public environment

Service Category: Physical Facility Services
Service Name: Robot test in public environment
Service Readiness: Available 1/1 2024
Service Type: Tailormade
Service Price: 14.700 DKK (based on an estimate of 20 confrontational hours). SMEs can use De Minimis state aid to cover 100 % of the service cost.

Service description: Do you want to test your robot technology in a real-life setting? In Aarhus, the entire city has potential to become your next digital playground. This service gives you access and assistance for robot tests in real-life settings and in close collaboration with the relevant municipal authorities. At Aarhus City Lab, we can offer you dedicated secured testing areas or a tailormade approach with the possibility to test all over the city of Aarhus as well as both urban and rural areas throughout the region of Central Denmark.  
For robots in their early development stages, we offer closed robot testing zones, where robots can be tested in a secured urban environment. The testing zone will be closed for pedestrians and other traffic, which means you can get an early proof of concept of your robot in a real environment. The primary geographical testing area is located by the habour in the City of Aarhus - however it is possible to make use of other locations in the urban environment (both indoor and outdoor) to better match your need. 
For robots with a more mature readiness level, we can provide a tailormade test zone in the public environment. We have access to very diverse public spaces from densely built areas to more rural surroundings. Aarhus City Lab will assist you in initiating dialogue with the city administration to find the right location for testing and obtain the permissions needed etc. 
With this service you also get access to a GNSS network for precise positioning (TAPAS) covering the city of Aarhus. TAPAS can provide documentation for autonomous units positioning system as it gives you 1 cm-level accuracy in real-time even in the most challenging urban environment. This makes Aarhus a reliable place for testing tracking solutions or similar. makes Aarhus a reliable place for testing tracking solutions or similar.
Through our municipally owned LoRaWAN setup (a Low Power, Wide Area networking protocol designed to wirelessly connect battery operated 'things' to the internet) you get access to our IoT Platform where your data can be collected, refined, stored, and visualized.
If you wish to conduct your test together with domain experts in Aarhus Municipality, we offer you our service 'Initiating GovTech Innovation'. Here, we explore the possibilities for relevant municipal cooperation and initiate a dialogue with you and the respective domain owners regarding testing in the city.

Service delivery process:
1.	Tells us about your company, test case and needs through our short questionnaire here: Testing with Aarhus City Lab
2.	Aarhus City Lab will contact you for further dialogue around the physical capabilities you need in your test (indoor, outdoor, pavement, obstacles etc.)
3.	If you wish to conduct your test together with domain experts in Aarhus Municipality, Aarhus City Lab will offer you the service ‘initiating GovTech Innovation’ prior to the test to match you with the best possible municipal partner.
4.	Aarhus City Lab will guide you through the application process with the Danish Road-Traffic Authorities to ensure the necessary permissions. Due to legal processing time, you should expect a minor fee from the Danish Government and a processing time of 1-2 months.
5.	If you need access to our GNSS network, IoT sensor data or the like we will make the necessary arrangements and onboarding.
Service deliverable: With this service you will be able to test your robot technology in the living city where the surface, weather, traffic, or obstacles might vary giving you a real life experience.
Service Demand: Relevant for all robotics companies and innovators who want to do real-life robotics testing.
Service Supply: Aarhus Municipality and the Gov Tech Midtjylland network.
Service Price: unit measurement: based on hours spent.
Service Compliance:
Service Competition: Aarhus is the only municipality in Denmark providing this service. The service is available in other countries, but legislation differs. We expect more competition in the early-stage robot tests in closed off areas as private car parks or similar can also be used. We see our main competitive advantage in the late-stage robot test where our service is the gateway to the entire city.
Service Availability: The service is available.

- **Service 2**: SERVICE 2: Create a custom IoT Smart City dataset for AI innovation

Service Category: Physical Facility Service + Virtual facility service
Service Name: Create a custom IoT Smart City dataset for AI innovation.
Service Readiness: Available 1/1 2024
Service Type: Tailormade
Service Price: 14.700 DKK (based on an estimate of 20 confrontational hours). SMEs can use De Minimis state aid to cover 100 % of the service cost.

Service Description: Do you need access to data from the living city in your product development? This service offers you the opportunity to use city data as the basis for your next AI innovation. With this service, AI innovators can, where urban data is missing, create their own custom IoT Smart City dataset. 
We will assist you in setting up your own sensors, radars, cameras etc. to make use of IoT infrastructure and gather customized datasets. In the process we will support you with the needed permissions from local authorities as well as partner you up with the right people within the municipality for potential further collaboration.
As the testing zone is not restricted to one area of the city, there are plenty of possibilities to customize the test project to meet your needs and set up sensors at street level, in the harbor, in or on buildings etc. 
Through our LoRaWAN setup (a Low Power, Wide Area networking protocol designed to wirelessly connect battery operated 'things' to the internet) you get access to our IoT Platform where your data can be collected, refined, stored, and visualized.
If you wish to conduct your test together with domain experts in Aarhus Municipality, we offer you our service 'Initiating GovTech Innovation'. Here, we explore the possibilities for relevant municipal cooperation and initiate a dialogue with you and the respective domain owners regarding testing in the city.

Service delivery process: 
1.	Tells us about your company, test case and needs through our short questionnaire here: Testing with Aarhus City Lab
2.	Further investigation and clarification with Aarhus City Lab to find the relevant physical surroundings best matching your needs. Aarhus City Lab will assist you in finding solutions that also meet guidelines and legal compliance. 
3.	If you wish to conduct your test together with domain experts in Aarhus Municipality, Aarhus City Lab will offer you the service ‘Initiating GovTech Innovation’ prior to the test to match you with the best possible municipal partner.
4.	Aarhus City Lab will guide you through the permission process with relevant authorities.
5.	Onboarding for access to IoT infrastructure

Service deliverable: Installation of sensors (or other relevant hardware) in the public space and access to IoT infrastructure
Service Demand: Relevant for all AI companies within EU, who want to do AI innovation based on Smart City data.
Service Supply: Aarhus Municipality and the Gov Tech Midtjylland network
